    How to play

    Beach Run: your duck sprints up the beach. Hold A or D (or the arrow keys) to run sideways, press Space to jump and Shift to sprint, a short invincible boost that smashes crabs and logs. On a phone, swipe left or right to move, swipe up to jump and use the Sprint button. Every point you earn is your score.

    The beach changes every 12 seconds: Grape Rain (follow the grape trail), Crab March (find the open lane), Rising Tide (jump the waves and grab the grapes in the air) and Golden Shower (big grapes, watch for logs).

    Every 24 seconds you pick one upgrade. Catch grapes without getting hit to build a combo up to x5, but it fades if you stop catching. Grab stars for double score and clocks for slow motion, dash through crabs to smash them, and clear a beach without a hit for a flawless bonus.

    Every mode is a different game: School Mode is a class quiz, Calm Tide is card matching, Night Dash is a flashlight hunt, Storm is crab smashing, Golden Hour is timing, Endless is pattern memory, Hardcore is sudden death reflexes and Grape Frenzy is grape popping. Classic and Grape Fishing work as described above.

    Spend grapes in the shop on skins, hats and crates. Every mode except Classic costs grapes, and a mode only shows up once you buy it in the shop (sign in first so it stays unlocked on your account). Grape Fishing is a mode where you cast a rod and click when the marker is in the green zone to reel in grape fish, but never click a crab. In parties pick Team Goal, Versus or Team A vs Team B. Party mode is co-op: scores add up. Buy traitor mode and one player secretly sabotages the team.
